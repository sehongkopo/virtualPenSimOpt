What the PPO policy sees

On-line channels recorded by the plant. The policy also reads the vessel weight and volume, off-gas O₂, the current feeds and the batch time.

What the controllers set

Sugar and PAA feeds actually applied. APC changes only the PAA feed. PPO also adjusts eight other variables, which stay within a few percent of the recipe.
