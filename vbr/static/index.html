Culture state PPO

A red blinking lamp means the variable needs action. PAA is above the toxic 2,400 mg/L, sugar is accumulating, biomass or penicillin is falling from its peak (the small mark on the dial), or DO and pH are far from normal. A yellow lamp means it is approaching that range. PAA, sugar, biomass and penicillin are not measured on-line, so PPO never sees them.
